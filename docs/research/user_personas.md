# User Personas: ReelUp Target Audience

## Persona 1: Marcus — The Commercial Videographer & Reels Creator
- **Profile**: 28 years old, shoots Sony A7S III / FX3 in 4K S-Log3, delivers client Reels, product showcases, and fashion clips.
- **Workflow**: Edits in DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro, exports 4K ProRes 422 HQ / high-bitrate H.264.
- **Frustration**: "My client Reels look razor sharp on my calibrated OLED monitor, but when uploaded to Instagram, skin tones lose depth and textures look like 720p mush."
- **Need**: A reliable delivery pipeline that preserves maximum perceptual sharpness and natural skin texture through Instagram's transcoding.

---

## Persona 2: Maya — The Solo Content Creator & Educator
- **Profile**: 24 years old, produces lifestyle, talking-head, and tutorial Reels with an iPhone 15 Pro and downloaded clips.
- **Workflow**: Edits directly in CapCut or on desktop, exports default settings.
- **Frustration**: "I don't understand CRF, GOP intervals, or bitrates. I just want my video to look crisp like the top influencers' Reels without spending hours tweaking settings."
- **Need**: One-click dropzone: upload video → automatic analysis → AI enhance to 4K → download file optimized for Instagram.

---

## Persona 3: Alex — Gaming & Motion Clip Creator
- **Profile**: 22 years old, edits fast-paced gaming highlights, screen recordings, and kinetic typography.
- **Workflow**: Captures OBS at 1080p 60fps, adds motion graphics and sound design.
- **Frustration**: "Fast motion in 60fps gaming clips completely disintegrates into macroblocking on Instagram Reels. Text becomes unreadable."
- **Need**: AI super-resolution capable of cleaning up compression artifacts and delivering motion-stable 60fps exports that survive platform bit-rate choking.

---

## Persona 4: Elena — Boutique Agency Social Media Manager
- **Profile**: 31 years old, manages Instagram accounts for 8 luxury & hospitality brands.
- **Workflow**: Coordinates multiple freelance video editors, handles daily uploads and asset approvals.
- **Frustration**: "Different editors send different formats: 1080p, 4K, 24fps, 60fps. Quality on our client feeds is inconsistent and clients complain."
- **Need**: A standardized platform to batch-process and normalize all client videos to a single, verified high-quality delivery standard.
